TESK API 1.3.0 brings the task fields, list filters and task states of TES 1.1, optional access for tokens of allow-listed service clients (e.g., a TES gateway that calls TESK with its client-credentials token), and experimental support for private executor images. Deployments need no changes, as the new options are off by default, but clients may notice changed answers, e.g., `CANCELING` before `CANCELED` and `400 Bad Request` for wildcard outputs without `path_prefix`: read the [upgrade notes](https://tesk.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tesk-api/upgrading/#to-130) before you upgrade. Uploading the matches of wildcard outputs needs a taskmaster whose filer supports `path_prefix` ([tesk-core#134](https://github.com/elixir-cloud-aai/tesk-core/pull/134)).

## Features

- Optional access for tokens of allow-listed service clients (OAuth 2.0 client credentials grant), checked locally against the issuer's keys, as principals of their own that create, list, get and cancel their own tasks; settings `TESK_API_AUTHORISATION_SERVICE_CLIENTS_*` (#153, #154)
- The task fields of TES 1.1 are accepted: `outputs[].path_prefix` is passed to the taskmaster, `inputs[].streamable` is ignored, `executors[].ignore_error` only as `false`, and `backend_parameters_strict: true` is refused; service info reports TES `1.1.0` (#133)
- `GET /tasks` filters by `state`, `tag_key` and `tag_value` (#143, #149)
- Tasks report `CANCELING` while the pods of a cancelled task still run, and `PREEMPTED` when Kubernetes disrupted all failed executors (#144, #151)
- `backend_parameters` are no longer stored or returned; ignored keys are logged and listed in `system_logs`, and service info lists `tesResources_backend_parameters` (`[]`) (#145, #147)
- Outputs with wildcards but no `path_prefix` are refused with `400 Bad Request`, unless they go to `file:` URLs, as TES 1.1 requires (#148, #152)
- Experimental: private executor images, with registry credentials in the executor fields `docker_registry`, `docker_login` and `docker_pass`, passed to Kubernetes as image-pull Secrets owned by the taskmaster's job and never returned (#55, #141)

## Fixes

- Inputs and outputs with `"type": null` are accepted as `FILE`, as when `type` is left out, as TES 1.1 allows (#146, #150)

## Maintenance
